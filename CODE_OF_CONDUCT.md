# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## In practice

Behaviour that is welcome here: giving specific, actionable feedback; assuming
good faith when a change is not what you would have written; saying plainly when
you do not know something.

Behaviour that is not: personal attacks, harassment of any kind, dismissing
someone rather than their argument, and deliberately wasting a reviewer's time.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where
someone is representing this project.

## Reporting

Open a private security advisory, or contact the maintainer directly. Reports
are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments,
and may block accounts, when this document is not being followed.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened because a document nobody reads protects nobody.
